# --description--

Right now, a `program` knows which department it belongs to by `departmentId`, but the department doesn't yet 
know which programs belong to it. Push each `program.id` into `departmentsById[departmentId].programIds`.

This creates a two-way relationship: the department can look up its programs through `programIds`, and 
each program can look up its department through `departmentId` — without either object needing to store 
the other's full data.

Log `departmentsById[departmentId]` in the console after `departmentData.programs` loop finishes.
