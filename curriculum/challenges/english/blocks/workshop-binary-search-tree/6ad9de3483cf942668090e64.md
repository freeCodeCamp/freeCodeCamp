The recursive calls go down the tree one level at a time, but they need a place to stop.

When the next node in the right direction doesn't exist, `_insert` is called with `None` as `node`. That's the empty spot where the new value belongs. Right now, your method would try to access `node.key` on `None` and raise an error.
